# Story Arcs — chapter & quest outlines per Reach

Companion to LORE.md (canon), THE-BOOK-OF-GRADUS.md (prose lore), and the chapter files (prose). This is the **narrative skeleton**: the story beats, twists, and character moments of each Reach. Game mechanics, objectives, and rewards are handled elsewhere; this file is the story.

Principle: each Reach (10 levels) is a full arc of ~5-7 chapters, not a single chapter. Every Reach should be rich enough that a reader/player stays engaged the whole way.

---

The Court (LOCKED): the realm of all the dead and the gods, regions under different Crowns, Caelvorn over all. Maelis died and went there (Seraphine hid her). Gradus did NOT die; he walked in alive, the only living soul ever to, which is his uniqueness.

## Book One throughline (Cassian, Reaches 1-5, levels 1-50) — THE SPINE

Everything below serves this. One protagonist, one propulsive line, the past braided in sparingly.

**Want (conscious):** Cassian wants to be worthy of the name his family cheapened, to make the legend true instead of sold, to matter. It looks like nobility. Under it is his wound: he once lost someone by trying to carry it alone, and he has sworn never to fail that way again, which has twisted into a need to carry everything himself.

**Need (the lesson he must learn):** that he cannot walk alone, and letting others in is not weakness. This is not a soft moral. It is survival, because the Hound turns vessels through isolation and despair (see Prologue, LORE 9g). His flaw is the exact trait that hollows a vessel.

**The central irony / engine:** his greatest virtue, the strong man who shoulders it all, is the road that walks straight to the Hollowed. He could become the next Roderic. Or he could be the first vessel to learn the thing even Gradus never learned. (Long-game resonance: Gradus, alone in the Court, cannot find Maelis because he will not stop and will not share the burden. Cassian's arc rhymes with the dead man's failure.)

**External goal:** down the Road toward the Bleeding Mile and the truth at the world's end, pulled by three cords braided tight: survival (he is marked, the Hound hunts him), the mystery (why did a god choose Aldred's heir, of all the souls in the world), and the cause (the Footsworn, the dream of breaking the Anathema).

**Stakes:** personal first (his soul, his hollowing, the people he fails), cosmic second (the Anathema, the frozen world). Always land the personal.

**The Book One question:** Will Cassian reach the end of the Road and the truth, or fall to the Hound and the despair the way Roderic and Brand did? And under it: can he learn to stop walking alone before it hollows him?

### Escalation by Reach

- **R1 Oppidum (inciting):** awakening, marked, the Hound loosed, he flees. Flaw seed: he insists on going into the Hollow alone; someone pays for his solitary heroics. (Arc detailed below.)
- **R2 Greenwood (rising):** the Footsworn rally to him and he resists being their symbol; he chooses his calling (lv10); first real enemies; he first hears the Second Strider sung as a warning. Flaw bites: he pushes allies away and it costs them.
- **R3 The Ruins (midpoint turn):** he meets Roderic, the fallen Second Strider, his living mirror, and is offered the rest. Midpoint reveal: the dead are not gone, there is a war in the Court. Cassian sees exactly the road he is on. The why-was-I-chosen mystery deepens.
- **R4 The Hoarmarch (all is lost):** grief made landscape, Wendholt and the Frozen Ford. His flaw causes a real and devastating loss, someone he would not let help. His wound reopens, despair takes him, and this is the closest he comes to hollowing, the Hound's true weapon at his throat. He may recover Maelis's Ring here.
- **R5 The Bleeding Mile (climax):** rage and the threshold. He faces Mordric and the horror of the Mile, and the true climax is a choice: take the rest the Hound offers and carry it alone into the dark forever (Roderic's road), or refuse the isolation and let someone in, which is the one thing that lets him resist the turning. He wins, if he wins, not by Gradus's force-and-solitude but by the thing Gradus never learned.

**Closing (Book One resolves, Book Two opens):** Cassian does not fall; he takes the first real step out of his isolation, and survives the trap that took Brand and Roderic. At the threshold he glimpses the giant secret, the dead are alive and Maelis is the hidden architect, and the door to the Sunless Court swings open. One arc closed (he learned not to walk alone), one vast door opened (the war in heaven, Book Two).

**Theme:** hope against despair; you cannot walk alone. Endurance is not enough. The lesson the legend never learned is the one that saves the man who carries him.

## The per-Reach template

1. **The Hook** — a dramatic opening event that sets the Reach's theme.
2. **The Ground** — the place, its people, the local faction, the player's footing; context.
3. **The Complication** — investigation; the problem deepens; the first twist.
4. **The Cost** — a loss or a hard truth; escalation; the second twist.
5. **The Reckoning** — the Reach's boss and confrontation.
6. **The Departure** — resolution, a new wound, the hook into the next Reach.

Threaded through every Reach: collectible **Accounts** (ACCOUNTS.md), quiet **interlude** glimpses of the dead (Gradus/Maelis), a recurring **warm/funny beat** (a tavern, an ember, a running gag, a moment the weight lifts, see VOICE-AND-CRAFT), and one **emotional register** per Reach (Oppidum: the fraying present and the terror and gift of waking up; Greenwood: freedom and feralness; Ruins: truth and the fallen; Hoarmarch: grief; Bleeding Mile: rage).

## Prologue — The Last Mile of Brand (prose: Chapter-00-Prologue.md)

The bang. A self-contained doom before Chapter One, GoT-prologue style: tight third on a doomed stranger (Brand, a vessel of Oppidum), hunted by the Iron Hound through a beautiful valley and turned into one of the Hollowed, from the inside. Establishes the villain, the supernatural threat, the gift, and the real horror (despair) with no exposition. Pays off in Chapter 5, when Brand returns Hollowed and the reader recognizes him. Set some years before Cassian.

## Reach 1 — Oppidum (levels 1-10): "The Waking Town"

Register: the fraying present, and the terror and gift of waking up. Six chapters, after the Prologue.

### Chapter 1 — The Dawn That Lied (prose: Chapter-01-The-Dawn-That-Lied.md)
The Hook. Cassian, six days in Oppidum, watches the dawn Litany order the town to wall its sick into the Hollow; he alone refuses; **Orris the Inkfinger** clocks him at the Inn; the **Vagabond** sends him to the Milestone, where the awakening fires; he defies **Vane** and goes down into the Hollow after the child. Ends on the descent (cliffhanger). (Introduces Maren, Hesta, Col, Orris, Vane, the Vagabond, the Milestone.)

### Chapter 2 — The Hollow (prose: Chapter-02-The-Hollow.md)
The Hook completes, and first blood for Cassian's flaw. He goes down alone (refuses to send the lad **Tam** back, decides he'll just protect everyone himself), fights and kills the **Tithe-Eater** with the awakening-strength, saves the child Col, but Tam dies because Cassian could not be everywhere at once. **Maren** at the mouth: "I'd have come. You only had to wait." Vane spins the death against him ("I told you it kept us safe"). The Vagabond names him a vessel of Gradus and warns him not to become the legend too quickly. That night the Litany speaks Cassian's name, and **Mordric the Iron Hound** turns toward the lake town and starts walking. The flaw seed of the whole book, paid in one life.

### Chapter 3 — The Marked
The Ground. Aftermath, with Tam freshly buried. Vane keeps denouncing him; the marked man is dangerous to know. The Footsworn cell, frightened people who have waited a lifetime, take Cassian in through Maren, though she is grieving the boy and her welcome is barbed. He learns the world: the Milestone, the coin, the Road, the creeping chill. Col trails him like a duckling, which Cassian cannot bear and cannot refuse. Quiet truth: a vessel is a symbol, and a symbol is a thing people use, and Cassian is already trying to carry the town's hope the same way he carried Tam's body, alone. Contains the **Foundered Horse** tavern beat (the warmth before the dread, now folded in from Scene-The-Foundered-Horse.md): the one room that won't let him carry it alone. Ends on the dread hook: a woman they saved from the Hollow found dead with the dreaming look, though the Tithe-Eater is dead, so something else told her to lie down.

### Chapter 4 — What the Well Fed
The Complication + first twist. The player digs into the Choir's grip and finds the machinery of the tithe: the dawn voice has been selecting people to feed the dark for years, with collaborators profiting at the edges. **Twist:** the Tithe-Eater was a buffer, kept sated so it would stay in its hole; killing it (the Chapter 1 "victory") has stirred the worse thing it was being fed to keep quiet, and given the Hound a clear scent. Vane was not only lying when he called sacrifice protection. He is a true believer who is partly right. The player's heroism had a cost.

### Chapter 5 — The Informer
The Cost + second twist. The Choir keeps moving a step ahead; someone is feeding them. **Twist:** it is **Edrin**, one of Maren's own, coerced because the Choir holds his daughter's safety over him. No clean answer: punish him and you are the Litany's kind of justice; spare him and the leak stays open. The cell fractures. Lesson: the resistance is not heroes but frightened people making impossible choices.

### Chapter 6 — The Returned
The Reckoning. The Hound's approach drives the first Hollowed into Oppidum, and it is personal: **Brand**, a vessel of Oppidum's own from years past, a beloved local who went down the Road to free the dead and was mourned as a martyr, come back corrupted and hollow-eyed, wearing the face the town still keeps a candle for. The fight is the small, devastating rehearsal of Roderic far down the Road: this is what a vessel can become, and the town's last hope curdles in front of them.

### Chapter 7 — The Long Way Out (prose: Chapter-07-The-Long-Way-Out.md)
The Departure (closes the Oppidum arc). Brand's second burial; Cassian resolves to leave so the Road stops coming to Oppidum. The ladies-man-but-innocent trait planted (the town's women fuss over him; he's oblivious; Maren's "jaw like a temple saint and the sense of a fence post"). Warm sendoff at the Foundered Horse (embers, the song-fight, Captain). The hard goodbye to Col (Cassian won't take a child onto the Road; entrusts him the coin, promises to come back for it). The Vagabond's cold edges shown plainly ("I am not your friend... a war bigger than you"). The Milestone hum older than the gods (Elder Road seed). And the companion: **Edra** joins him at the gate so he does not walk out alone (first proof of his arc). Hook: they take the Road south toward the Greenwood, the cold deepening, a name in a song pulling onward. (Class-choosing held for Reach 2.)
