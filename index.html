📖 What is Gradus?▾

Gradus is a medieval RPG powered by your real-world fitness.

In the world of Viatera, the dawn voice that once blessed every life has turned cruel, and toil now only drains the faithful. You are the exception: a vessel, whose effort returns as power. Every step you walk becomes Gradus — the coin struck in the name of the one mortal who ever defied a god and was not broken. Every calorie burned becomes ⚡ Ries, the breath of that same defiance.

Buy gear. Take quests. Fight monsters. Walk the Road. Build your wanderer — one step at a time.

⚔️ How does it work?▾

1 step = 1 Gradus · ⚡ 1 calorie = 1 Ries

Go to Realm Sync each day and enter your steps and calories (or link your Apple Watch / Oura Ring / Garmin for automatic sync).

Hit 7,000 steps daily to earn a streak bonus. Walk more, earn more, go further.

🏰 The World of Oppidum▾

You begin in Oppidum — a small walled waystation on a grey lake at the head of the ancient Road, where the dawn Litany has begun to lie.

Take quests at the Foundered Horse, arm yourself at the Blacksmith, rest at Maren's, store your coin at the Bank before a dangerous hunt, and lay your real steps on the Milestone at the Keep. Defeat a Reach's boss to take the Road on to the next.

"The gate's always open, wanderer. Whether you come back through it — that's up to you."

❓ Quick FAQ▾

Is it free? Yes, completely.

Does it track me automatically? Only if you connect a device. Otherwise you enter your steps manually.

Do I need to walk every day? No — but the streak bonus (up to +50% earnings) rewards consistency.

Will I lose my progress if I stop walking? Your gear and level stay. Only your daily earnings depend on steps.

Can I play with friends? Multiplayer is on the roadmap. For now, compare your wanderer's progress!

📖 How to Play

New here? You don't need the story to play. Just follow the loop.

The core loop: Walk → Sync → Earn → Spend → Grow. Walk in real life, sync your steps & calories, spend the currency on quests and gear, and your hero grows stronger.

🪙 The two currencies

🪙 Gradus — coin, from steps. 1 step = 1 Gradus. Spend it on gear, repairs, and rooms. Warning: Gradus you're carrying is lost if your hero dies — coin in the Bank is safe.

⚡ Ries — energy, from calories. 1 calorie = 1 Ries (cap 5,000). Every quest, hunt, and forge upgrade costs Ries. Out? Move in real life or eat a cooked meal.

📜 Realm Sync — do this first

Open Realm Sync at the Keep and enter (or simulate) today's steps and calories — that's how real activity becomes currency. Limits: 50,000 steps / 4,000 kcal per day; you can back-fill the last 2 days.

Daily goal 7,000 steps pays a bonus (+2,000 Gradus, +200 Ries) and extends your streak. 10,000 adds +1,500 Gradus. Each streak day adds +5% earnings, up to +50%.

🏰 The town — what each building does

Keep — Realm Sync. Foundered Horse — quests & fights. Maren's (Inn) — heal, rent a room (restores Ries + a quest slot), hear rumors. Bank — store coin safely + investments. Blacksmith/Woodsmith/Armorer — buy & repair gear. Apothecary — potions & enchantments. Hunt — free grinding. Lake/Commons — life skills. Library — this guide & the lore.

⚔️ Combat basics

Fights are turn-based: Attack, class ability (cooldown), Potion (buy at the Apothecary first), or Flee. Every fight costs Ries.

Monster traits: ⚡Swift strikes first · 🛡️Armored cuts your damage · ☠️Venom bleeds you · 😡Frenzy rages below 30% · 💚Regen self-heals.

Death loses all carried Gradus and wounds you (rest at the Inn). Always bank before a boss.

🧭 Your character & quests

You start as a Wanderer. At level 10 you pick a class (Knight, Mage, Archer, Cleric). Spend stat points under Me → Stats.

Quests pay once; Free Hunts are unlimited grinding (Ries only); Dungeons are level-gated delves that drop unique relics. Can't take a quest? You're wounded, out of Ries, or it's still a locked rumor.

The Accounts & Glossary

The Realms
You play in Viatera, the Trodden Lands: Oppidum, the Road, the Reaches. Your body walks in the Realm of Toil, the world with sky. For a thousand years the Litany blessed every life and toil drained no one; now the dawn voice lies, and most who toil only tire. You are the exception: your effort returns as power. Every step taken in the Realm of Toil becomes Gradus here; every calorie burned becomes Ries. (A third realm, the Sunless Court of the dead and the gods, waits past the Road's end.)
Gradus
The realm's coin, struck in the name of the one mortal who ever defied a god and was not broken — the name the High Crown swore to erase. To earn and spend it is a small act of defiance. 1 step in the Realm of Toil = 1 Gradus. Looters take all carried Gradus if you fall; the Bank's vault is safe.
Ries ⚡
From the old tongue ríes, "breath" or "spent fire": the energy of the same defiance. 1 calorie burned = 1 Ries, held to a cap of 5,000. Fuels quests, free hunts, life skills, crafting, and forge upgrades. Runs dry? Toil, or eat a cooked meal. Sits full? You're wasting breath. Spend it.
Realm Sync 📜
The Keep's ledger where the other realm's deeds are recorded: by hand, or automatically through a linked device (Garmin, Oura and kin). Daily limits: 50,000 steps, 4,000 kcal. Consecutive days build a streak worth up to +50% earnings.
Gear grades
No-grade gear is available from the start. D-grade drops in the Greenwood (zone 2). Higher grades (C → B → A → S) will arrive with future zones. The forge can raise any piece to +5.
Monster traits
⚡Swift strikes first · 🛡️Armored blunts your blade · ☠️Venom bleeds you each turn · 😡Frenzy rages below 30% health · 💚Regen knits its wounds.
Deeds & Titles 🏅
Feats of both realms earn deeds; deeds grant titles worn beside your name.
Letters of Investment 📜
Seal Gradus with the Bank for 7 days (+3%) or 30 days (+5%). Sealed coin cannot be touched, by you or by looters.
Rumors ❓
Some hunts are only learned of in the Inn's common room. Buy a round; listen well.
Free hunts 🌾
Each zone keeps open ground where mobs roam. No quest needed, no daily limit: only Ries. The proving grounds of high-level wanderers.
Dungeons ⛏️
Multi-fight delves beneath the realm, each gated by level. The first clear of a dungeon yields a unique relic: gear found nowhere else. Relics appear under Dungeon Finds in your inventory.
Equipment slots 🛡️
Seven places to wear steel: weapon, shield, helm, armor, gloves, pants, boots. Every piece adds to your strength; the paperdoll shows what you wear.
Durability 🔧
Gear wears down as you fight. Worn gear protects poorly; the Blacksmith repairs it for coin. Check your equipment before a boss.

FAQ

How do I get rich?
Walk. The realm pays 1 Gradus per step, plus streak bonuses. Quests pay once; the road pays every day.
What is the wanderer's daily minimum?
7,000 steps — the road's daily toll. Hit it to advance your sync streak (+5% per day, up to +50%) and earn a 🎯 bonus of +2,000G and +200R. Push to 10,000 steps for an extra ⭐ 1,500G. Your daily login reward also scales with yesterday's effort.
I died and lost everything.
You lost everything you carried. Bank before bosses. The Inn will mend you.
Why can't I take quests?
You are wounded (rest at the Inn), out of Ries (move in the other realm), or the hunt is still a rumor. Renting a room at the Inn restores Ries and adds a bonus quest slot.
How do I open the Greenwood?
Lay Brand the Returned to rest at the end of the Oppidum board, then take the Road south.
What should I spend Ries on?
Hunts and the forge, mostly. High-level wanderers live in the free hunts; their Ries is never idle.
