## The core loop **[core]**

Gradus turns your real-world activity into a medieval RPG. The whole game is one loop:

**Walk → Sync → Earn → Spend → Grow.**

1. **Walk** in real life (steps and calories).
2. **Sync** them at the Wayward Keep — steps become **Gradus** (coin), calories become **Ries** (energy).
3. **Spend** them: take quests, fight monsters, buy and upgrade gear.
4. **Grow** your hero — level up, get stronger, unlock new zones.

The more you move in real life, the more powerful your hero becomes. That's the deal.

*Flavor: two realms — the world with sky where your body walks, and Viatera where your effort returns as power.*

---

## The two currencies **[core]**

**🪙 Gradus — your coin. Earned from steps.**
1 step = 1 Gradus. You spend it on gear, repairs, rooms, and most things you buy. **Careful:** any Gradus you're *carrying* is lost if your hero dies in battle. Money kept in the Bank vault is safe.
*Flavor: struck in the name of the one mortal who ever defied a god.*

**⚡ Ries — your energy. Earned from calories burned.**
1 calorie = 1 Ries (capped at 5,000). It fuels actions: every quest, hunt, dungeon fight, life skill, and forge upgrade costs Ries. Out of Ries? Move in real life, or eat a cooked meal. Full on Ries? You're wasting it — go spend it.
*Flavor: from the old word for "breath" or "spent fire."*

## Realm Sync — getting your activity in **[core]**

Open **Realm Sync** at the Wayward Keep (the Milestone). This is where real activity becomes in-game currency. **Do this first** — it's the start of every play session.

- **Manual entry:** type in today's steps and calories.
- **Device sync:** link a fitness device (Garmin, Oura, and similar) to push automatically.
- **Daily limits:** 50,000 steps and 4,000 kcal per day.
- **Back-entry:** you can fill in the last 2 days if you missed them.

**Daily goals & streaks:**
- **7,000 steps** is the daily goal. Hit it to earn a bonus (+2,000 Gradus, +200 Ries) *and* extend your streak.
- **10,000 steps** earns an extra ⭐ +1,500 Gradus.
- **Streaks:** each consecutive 7K+ day adds **+5% earnings**, up to **+50%**. Miss a day and it resets.
- Your daily login reward also scales with how much you moved yesterday.

The town map is your hub. Tap a building to enter.

| Building | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Wayward Keep / Milestone** | Realm Sync — turn steps & calories into currency. |
| **The Foundered Horse** (tavern) | Quest board — accept quests and start fights. |

## Combat basics **[core]**

Fights are **turn-based**. On your turn you choose:

- **Attack** — your basic hit.
- **Class ability** — a stronger move on a cooldown (once you've picked a class).
- **Potion** — heal mid-fight (buy these at the Apothecary first).
- **Flee** — escape a fight that's going badly.

**Every fight costs Ries.** Watch for **monster traits**:
- ⚡ **Swift** — strikes first.
- 🛡️ **Armored** — reduces your damage.
- ☠️ **Venom** — bleeds you each turn.
- 😡 **Frenzy** — rages when below 30% health.
- 💚 **Regen** — heals its own wounds.

**Death:** if your hero falls, you lose all the Gradus you were *carrying* and get wounded. Rest at the Inn to recover. **Always bank your coin before a boss fight.**

## Your character **[core]**

- You start as a **Wanderer**, with gender and hair sprite options.
- At **level 10** you choose a **class**: Knight, Mage, Archer, or Cleric. Each has a passive, a weapon affinity (+10% with its weapon type), and an active battle ability.
- **Stat points** are awarded on level-up — spend them under **Me → Stats** (VIT and others).

---

## Gear & the Forge

- **7 equipment slots:** weapon, shield, helm, armor, gloves, pants, boots. Every piece adds to your strength; the paperdoll shows what you're wearing.
- **Grades:** No-grade → D → C → B → A → S. No-grade is available from the start; D-grade drops in the Greenwood (zone 2); higher grades arrive with future zones.
- **The Forge** (Blacksmith) raises any piece up to **+5** using Ries.
- **Durability:** gear wears down as you fight and protects worse when worn. The Blacksmith repairs it for coin — check before a boss.
- **Enchantments** (Apothecary) add a permanent +10% to a piece.

---

## Quests, Free Hunts & Dungeons

- **Quests** (Foundered Horse) — Hunt / Contract / Boss / Legend. Some are repeatable, some are one-time story quests. Each pays out once. A few are *rumors* — you only unlock them by buying a round at the Inn and listening.
- **Free Hunts** (Hunting Grounds) — open grinding areas, one per zone. No quest needed, no daily limit — they only cost Ries. Where high-level players live.
- **Dungeons** — multi-fight delves, each gated by level (level 5 up to 55). The first time you clear one, you get a unique **relic** (gear found nowhere else), listed under Dungeon Finds in your inventory.

**Why can't I take a quest right now?** Usually one of: you're wounded (rest at the Inn), you're out of Ries (go move), or the quest is still a locked rumor.

## Life skills & crafting

Train these independently of your combat level:

- **Fishing** (Lake), **Woodcutting / Thieving** (Commons), **Gathering**, **Cooking** (crafting bench).
- **Crafting** combines mob drops (herbs, hides, venom sacs, glowdust, timber) plus Ries into potions, meals, and enchantments. Cooked meals also restore Ries.

---

## The Bank & investments

- The **vault** keeps Gradus safe — looters can't take banked coin if you die. **Bank before bosses.**
- **Letters of Investment** seal coin for interest: **7 days for +3%**, or **30 days for +5%**. Sealed coin can't be touched by you *or* by looters until it matures.

## Quick FAQ **[core]**

**How do I get rich?** Walk. The realm pays 1 Gradus per step, plus streak bonuses. Quests pay once; walking pays every day.

**I died and lost everything!** You lost what you were *carrying*. Bank your coin before bosses; the Inn will heal you.

**What should I spend Ries on?** Mostly hunts and the Forge. Don't let it sit at the cap.

**How do I unlock the Greenwood?** Beat Brand the Returned at the end of the Oppidum board, then take the Road south.

**I'm confused by the story.** That's fine — you don't need the lore to play. Follow the loop (walk → sync → quest → fight → upgrade). The story is optional flavor in the tavern, Chapel, and Library Accounts.
