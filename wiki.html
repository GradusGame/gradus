The core loop

Walk → Sync → Earn → Spend → Grow

Gradus turns your real-world activity into a medieval RPG:

1. Walk in real life (steps and calories). 2. Sync them at the Keep — steps become Gradus (coin), calories become Ries (energy). 3. Spend them on quests, fights, and gear. 4. Grow your hero — level up, get stronger, unlock new zones. The more you move in real life, the more powerful your hero becomes.

Two realms: the world with sky where your body walks, and Viatera where your effort returns as power.

The two currencies

🪙 Gradus — your coin. Earned from steps.

1 step = 1 Gradus. Spend it on gear, repairs, rooms, and most things you buy. Careful: any Gradus you're carrying is lost if your hero dies in battle. Money kept in the Bank vault is safe.

Struck in the name of the one mortal who ever defied a god.

⚡ Ries — your energy. Earned from calories burned.

1 calorie = 1 Ries (capped at 5,000). It fuels actions: every quest, hunt, dungeon fight, life skill, and forge upgrade costs Ries. Out of Ries? Move in real life, or eat a cooked meal. Full on Ries? You're wasting it — go spend it.

From the old word for "breath" or "spent fire."

Realm Sync — getting your activity in

Open Realm Sync at the Wayward Keep (the Milestone). This is where real activity becomes currency. Do this first — it's the start of every play session.

Manual entryType in today's steps and calories.
Device syncLink a fitness device (Garmin, Oura, and similar) to push automatically.
Daily limits50,000 steps and 4,000 kcal per day.
Back-entryYou can fill in the last 2 days if you missed them.

Daily goals & streaks

7,000 steps is the daily goal — hit it for a bonus (+2,000 Gradus, +200 Ries) and to extend your streak. 10,000 steps earns an extra +1,500 Gradus. Each consecutive 7K+ day adds +5% earnings, up to +50%; miss a day and it resets. Your daily login reward also scales with how much you moved yesterday.

Combat basics

Fights are turn-based. On your turn you choose:

AttackYour basic hit.
Class abilityA stronger move on a cooldown (once you've picked a class).
PotionHeal mid-fight (buy these at the Apothecary first).
FleeEscape a fight that's going badly.

Every fight costs Ries. Watch for monster traits:

⚡ SwiftStrikes first.
🛡️ ArmoredReduces your damage.
☠️ VenomBleeds you each turn.
😡 FrenzyRages when below 30% health.
💚 RegenHeals its own wounds.

Death: if your hero falls, you lose all the Gradus you were carrying and get wounded. Rest at the Inn to recover. Always bank your coin before a boss fight.

Your character

You start as a Wanderer, with gender and hair options. At level 10 you choose a class: Knight, Mage, Archer, or Cleric. Each has a passive, a weapon affinity (+10% with its weapon type), and an active battle ability. Stat points are awarded on level-up — spend them under Me → Stats.

Gear & the Forge

7 equipment slots: weapon, shield, helm, armor, gloves, pants, boots. Every piece adds to your strength; the paperdoll shows what you're wearing.

Grades: No-grade → D → C → B → A → S. No-grade is available from the start; D-grade drops in the Greenwood (zone 2); higher grades arrive with future zones.

The Forge (Blacksmith) raises any piece up to +5 using Ries. Durability: gear wears down as you fight and protects worse when worn — the Blacksmith repairs it for coin, so check before a boss. Enchantments (Apothecary) add a permanent +10% to a piece.

Quests, Free Hunts & Dungeons

Quests (Foundered Horse) — Hunt / Contract / Boss / Legend. Some repeatable, some one-time story quests; each pays out once. A few are rumors — unlock them by buying a round at the Inn and listening.

Free Hunts (Hunting Grounds) — open grinding areas, one per zone. No quest needed, no daily limit — they only cost Ries. Where high-level players live.

Dungeons — multi-fight delves, each gated by level (5 up to 55). First clear gives a unique relic (gear found nowhere else), listed under Dungeon Finds in your inventory.

Why can't I take a quest right now? Usually: you're wounded (rest at the Inn), out of Ries (go move), or the quest is still a locked rumor.

Life skills & crafting

Train these independently of your combat level: Fishing (Lake), Woodcutting / Thieving (Commons), Gathering, and Cooking (crafting bench). Crafting combines mob drops (herbs, hides, venom sacs, glowdust, timber) plus Ries into potions, meals, and enchantments. Cooked meals also restore Ries.

↑ top

The Bank & investments

The vault keeps Gradus safe — looters can't take banked coin if you die. Bank before bosses. Letters of Investment seal coin for interest: 7 days for +3%, or 30 days for +5%. Sealed coin can't be touched by you or by looters until it matures.

Quick FAQ

How do I get rich? Walk. The realm pays 1 Gradus per step, plus streak bonuses. Quests pay once; walking pays every day.

I died and lost everything! You lost what you were carrying. Bank your coin before bosses; the Inn will heal you.

What should I spend Ries on? Mostly hunts and the Forge. Don't let it sit at the cap.

How do I unlock the Greenwood? Beat Brand the Returned at the end of the Oppidum board, then take the Road south.

I'm confused by the story. That's fine — you don't need the lore to play. Follow the loop (walk → sync → quest → fight → upgrade). The story is optional flavor in the tavern, Chapel, and Library Accounts.
