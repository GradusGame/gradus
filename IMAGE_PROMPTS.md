# Gradus — Grok Imagine Prompt Library

Style baseline for every prompt (append to all unless noted):
> **16-bit pixel art, medieval fantasy, crisp clean pixel outlines, limited color palette, no anti-aliasing, retro RPG game aesthetic, transparent or solid dark background.**

## 1. TOWN OVERVIEW

### 1a. Oppidum — Day
A wide top-down isometric pixel art town scene in a crisp 16-bit style. The town is fully visible. In the center stands a grand medieval **castle keep** with stone walls and crenellated battlements. Surrounding it on cobblestone streets: a **bank** with a coin emblem above an iron door, a warm **tavern** with a hanging boar sign and barrels outside, a **blacksmith** with a glowing forge and anvil, an **armorer's shop** with shields and plate on display, a **woodsmith** with bows and staves in the window, an **apothecary** with herb bundles and colorful potion bottles, an **inn** with a lit lantern above the door, a **chapel** with a small stone spire, a **library** with stacked tomes visible through the window, a **commons** area with market stalls and crates, a **crafting bench** shed, a path leading to a **forest edge**, and a path to a **lake**. NPCs, torches, banners, carts, and a stray dog scattered throughout. Warm earthy tones with vibrant accents. Isometric view, high detail, game UI style. 16-bit pixel art, medieval fantasy, crisp pixel outlines, no anti-aliasing.

### 1b. Oppidum — Night
Same composition as the day version but set at night. Deep navy and indigo sky with pixel stars and a large full moon. The castle is moonlit with lit arrow-slit windows. The tavern glows amber from within, smoke curling from the chimney. The blacksmith's forge spills orange-red light into the street. The apothecary has an eerie blue-green potion glow. The chapel window glows with soft candlelight. Torches, lanterns, and fireflies dot the cobblestone paths. Deep blues, purples, blacks with warm orange and cool silver light sources. Isometric view. 16-bit pixel art, medieval fantasy, atmospheric night lighting, crisp pixel outlines, no anti-aliasing.
